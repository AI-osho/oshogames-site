See it move

Four fighters, one stage, no health bars. Damage builds a percentage — the higher it climbs, the further the next hit sends you flying.

What's in it

Everything is drawn like a comic book now: BAM! and POW! on the big hits, and a POP! for every knockout.

Versus

You against up to three CPU fighters at four difficulty levels, on four stages. Stocks from one to five, or endless.

Quest

Solo: four chapters, boss fights, gear and skills.

NEW

Daily mini-game

A new game every day: SKY HIGH (launch POPPY as far as you can) and BALLOON POP (pop ten balloons against the clock) take turns. The same fighter for everyone that day.

Weekly challenge

The same fighter, the same eight opponents and the same stages for everyone that week. Beat as many as you can, earn a bronze, silver or gold medal, and race this week's bot. Send a friend a challenge code for the same eight fights.

Weekly ranking

Ordered by fighters beaten, then time. Every entry is replayed on our server before it is listed, and you can watch the top run. Seeker owners also get a board of their own.

Optional — the whole game plays without it.
NEW

Fighter records

Ten goals for each fighter. Unlock a name tag, an alternate color and a gold frame — looks only, never power.

Controls

One stick and four buttons — attack, special, guard and jump. Change their size, opacity and position, or switch to a left-handed layout, and try it in the practice room. Vibration can be turned off.

Eight fighters

Two humans, two robots, two beasts and two monsters — RONIN, VOLT, PISTON, BLIP, FANG, TUSK, GLOOM and HORN. Each fighter's specials have a look of their own: ink, lightning, steam, pixels, claws, rock, slime and fire.
