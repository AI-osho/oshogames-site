3. Wallet connection — optional, and never financial

You may connect a Solana wallet. It is entirely optional: the game is complete without it, and nothing is locked behind it.

Connecting a wallet costs nothing and moves nothing. The game does not process payments, does not use or issue tokens or NFTs, and never asks your wallet to approve a transaction or to write anything to any blockchain. The only thing it ever asks your wallet to do is sign a short message proving you own the address, and only at the moment you submit to the ranking.

Coins, shards and equipment inside the game are ordinary game data stored on your own device. They have no monetary value, cannot be bought, sold, transferred or exchanged, and are not property. Connecting a wallet does not change this.

3b. The weekly ranking

The weekly challenge can be played offline like the rest of the game. Submitting your record to the ranking is a separate, optional action that uses the internet and a wallet (if none is connected, it is connected at that moment). What is sent is set out in the Privacy Policy.

Entries are ranked by how many fighters were beaten, then by time. A run counts if it beats at least one fighter. Each version of the game has its own board. The recorded runs of the top entries can be watched by other players in the game. The Seeker-only board lists entries from wallets that held a Seeker Genesis Token when they were submitted, one entry per token.

To keep the board meaningful, every submitted record is re-played on our server and checked against what you claimed. Entries that do not reproduce are not listed. We may remove names or entries that are offensive, impersonate someone, or come from copying another player's recorded run or from cheating — including modifying the game, automating play, or any other means that misrepresents how the record was achieved — and we may stop accepting submissions from the wallets involved.

The name shown next to your record is the display name your wallet reports. If a name is offensive or impersonates someone, we may hide or remove it. We may also change how the ranking works, reset a week, or discontinue the ranking entirely; the rest of the game is unaffected if we do.

The ranking is a free service provided as-is. We do not guarantee that it will be available, that an entry will be kept, or that it will be free of error.
