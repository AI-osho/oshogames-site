Destrina POW

Wreck the whole town!

A demolition party game on Roblox. Join Red or Blue and smash buildings, castles and giant monsters — up to 8 players a server.

How it plays

Play Red vs Blue, or Free mode to smash with no teams. Everyone votes on the stage — seven of them, from a big city to a robot factory. A round takes about two and a half minutes.

Demolition Battle

Score by smashing the town and by stealing points with prank weapons. The team with the higher total wins. CPUs fill any empty spots.

Castle Battle

Break the core of the enemy castle before they break yours. With 45 seconds left, the castles stand up!

The town fights back

Smash enough and the town gets angry: flying foes, giants and the town's boss. Break every weak part of a giant monster to bring it down.

Grow giant

Grab a capsule as it falls from the sky and grow as tall as a building. There are human cannons, a turf grab and light moon gravity too.

Treasure pals

Carry treasure capsules back to your base. Treasure pals line up on your shelf and make coins — spend them on weapons, rides and looks, and upgrade in the workshop.

Weekends

On Saturdays and Sundays (Japan time), treasure capsules are doubled and giant monsters show up more often. Around 12:00 and 20:00, the Moonlit Raid puts Red and Blue on the same side against the Cloud Whale.
