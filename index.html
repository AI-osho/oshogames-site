OUT NOW

POP CLASH

Eight fighters, one arena. Damage builds a percentage instead of draining a health bar — the higher yours climbs, the further the next hit sends you. Version 2.0 redraws everything like a comic book and adds a JUMP button, a new mini-game every day, weekly medals and a bot to beat. The weekly ranking and the wallet stay optional, and the whole game plays offline.

  • Android
  • Platform fighter
  • Plays offline
  • vs CPU
  • 8 languages
  • Free
  • Weekly ranking

GUNZ STRIKE

A run and gun you can play with one hand: you advance and fire on your own while your thumb steers. Break open cocoons to rescue survivors, recruit partners — two fight beside you at a time — and push through infected zones, each with its own boss, to the hive at the end.

  • One hand
  • Voxel
  • 4 languages
