OUT NOW

POP CLASH

Eight fighters. One arena. No health bars — every hit pushes your percentage up, and the higher it climbs, the farther the next one sends you flying. Version 3.0 is a full native rebuild: combo stamps, a VS cover before every match and a comic-book spread for the final blow. Plus a new mini-game every day, weekly medals and a bot to beat. The whole game plays offline; the weekly ranking and the wallet are optional.

  • Android
  • Platform fighter
  • Plays offline
  • Vs. CPU
  • 8 languages
  • Free
  • Weekly ranking

CUBE SQUAD

Developed under the name GUNZ STRIKE.

Drag to move. Your squad does the shooting. A one-thumb action shooter built from chunky voxels — take up to two partners through 13 sectors of the quarantine zone, crack the cocoons to free survivors, choose from 18 weapons and awaken them, then storm the Hive. The whole game plays offline; the wallet only unlocks an optional perk for Seeker owners.

UNDERDIGS

Developed under the name RELIC DIVER.

A 3D heist puzzle, one dig at a time. Lead a crew of thieves through five areas beneath NEW BULLION CITY. The guard robots move only when you do — so every move is yours to plan. The story plays offline, and every day a new Chain Vault is born from Solana's first block of the day.

DESTRINA POW

Japanese title: デストリーナPOW

Wreck the whole town! Join Red or Blue and smash buildings, castles and giant monsters in a demolition party game for up to eight players. Vote on the stage, grab a falling capsule to grow as tall as a building, and on weekends team up against the Cloud Whale in the Moonlit Raid. Collect treasure pals and spend the coins you earn by playing on gear and upgrades.

  • Roblox
  • PC · Phone · Tablet
  • Party game
  • Up to 8 players
  • Free to play
  • In-game text: Japanese
