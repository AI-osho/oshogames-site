The story

One hundred days ago, something swallowed the front line and started rebuilding the world into a nest, one block at a time: fields into flesh, towns into cocoons, tanks into beasts. The army that went in first never came back.

Now a tiny unit with a long official name, C.U.B.E., goes in instead, with museum-piece guns, a bomber older than its pilot and a squad leader who keeps getting busted back to private for disobeying the order to retreat.

Break the nests. Free your people. Take the Hive.

How it plays

CUBE SQUAD is a one-thumb action shooter in a chunky voxel style. As the squad leader, you take up to two partners through the 13 sectors of the quarantine zone: from the Grassland to Ground Zero, and on into the Hive, where the Matriarch waits. Firing is automatic. You decide where to stand, which weapon to take and when to call in the air strike.

13 sectors, 13 bosses

Every boss has a trick. Read it, and hit hard when WEAK POINT appears.

18 weapons

From old pistols to a rocket launcher. Pick 1 of 3 from each supply crate, level them up and awaken them: blue, purple, orange.

Fusion and a second lap

Reach the Hive once to unlock weapon fusion and a second lap through the Rot's own lands, with new sectors and new bosses.

9 survivors to free

Break the cocoons to free them. Partners fight on their own: a medic, a heavy gunner, a sniper, a drone pilot, a flame trooper and more.

Promotions and perks

Get promoted every 5 levels and choose a perk.

Weekly operations

Operations and badges earn medals, tickets and new looks. Every look carries a small bonus.
