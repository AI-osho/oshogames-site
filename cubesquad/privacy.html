CUBE SQUAD — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 12 October 2026 (version 1.0)

CUBE SQUAD plays entirely offline. There is no account and no sign-in. Connecting a Solana wallet is optional and is used for one thing only: checking whether you own a Seeker, to unlock a free perk weapon. Everything else in the game works with the network switched off.

What the app collects on its own

Nothing. There is no analytics, no advertising, no crash reporting and no third-party SDK that transmits data. The game engine's diagnostic data and hardware statistics are turned off. The app declares internet access and network-state access, and uses them only for the optional wallet connection and Seeker check described below (on Android, even talking to a wallet app on the same device counts as internet access). Nothing is sent anywhere unless you choose to connect a wallet.

What the app stores on your device

One save file, held in the app's own private storage. It contains your progress (your furthest point and best score, whether you have cleared the second loop, how many runs you have played, the last day you played, the difficulty you chose and whether you have seen the first-time guide), your coins and upgrades, your partners and their levels, the outfits you own and wear, your weekly challenge progress and badges, and your settings (language, graphics quality and volume). Weapons and rank are earned within a single run and are not saved.

The app does not keep play logs or performance records on your device.

If you connect a wallet and the Seeker check succeeds, the app also stores the result on your device: your wallet's public address, the address of the Seeker Genesis Token and the time of the check, together with whether you have seen the perk notice and whether you chose to start runs with the perk weapon.

None of this leaves your device through the app. Uninstalling the app removes it. Depending on your device settings, Android may include the app's data in your own device backup (for example, a backup to your Google account, or a transfer to a new phone). That backup is handled by Android and your Google account; it never reaches OSHO GAMES.

Connecting a wallet (optional)

Connecting uses the Solana Mobile Wallet Adapter, which talks to a wallet app installed on the same device. We never see your private key or seed phrase. The app never asks your wallet to approve a transaction, spend anything, or write to any blockchain. The authorisation your wallet gives the app is kept only in memory while the app is open, and the app does not reconnect to your wallet automatically next time.

Seeker owner perk (optional)

When you connect a wallet, the app asks Solana's public RPC endpoint (api.mainnet-beta.solana.com, or api.mainnet.solana.com if that cannot be reached) whether the wallet holds a Seeker Genesis Token. The first request sends the wallet's public address; the second sends the public addresses of the tokens found in that wallet (split into batches of 100 if there are many). This happens only if the check has not already been completed on that device. If the wallet holds a Seeker Genesis Token, the app asks your wallet to sign a short message (no transfer, no fee) to confirm that you own the wallet. The message names the game and the check, and contains a one-time random value and the time. The signature is checked on your device and is not sent anywhere. The result (token address, wallet address and the time of the check) is stored only on your device.

A wallet's public address and its tokens are already public on the Solana blockchain. As with any request over the internet, the RPC service can see your device's IP address; it is operated by a third party, not by OSHO GAMES.

Who else receives anything

OSHO GAMES runs no server for this game and receives nothing from it. There is no analytics provider, no advertising network and no data broker. The only outside service the app contacts is the public Solana RPC service described above, and only when you connect a wallet.

Children

Played on its own, the game collects nothing, so it collects nothing from children. The Seeker owner perk requires a Solana wallet. There is no chat, messaging or other way for players to communicate.

Changes

If a future version collects anything not described here, this page will be updated before that version is released, and the change will be described here.
