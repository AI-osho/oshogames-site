The story

NEW BULLION CITY is built on gold, and the gold belongs to a few very loud people. Underneath it all — in the subway, the old flood tunnels, an underground mall, a data vault and a vain billionaire's private base — the city's treasures sit behind locks, guard robots and a great deal of showing off.

UNDERDIGS are a small crew of one-of-a-kind thieves. Guided by Granny Kiyo's voice on the radio, they dig in from below, take the treasure piece by piece and follow the trail of five fragments toward the secret of the founder's first vault.

Dig in. Steal it back. Nobody gets hurt.

How it plays

Each mission is a small 3D room seen from above. Dig through the dirt one block at a time, climb, carry the parts back out — and time it so the guard robots never catch you. They only move when you act, so there is always time to think.

Five areas, 100 missions

Twenty missions in each area: the main heists, harder back-room jobs and a showdown at the end.

Five villain showdowns

Every area ends with a showdown in three acts against a villain with a signature trick of their own.

A crew with skills

Switch between crew members, and use each one's special move at the right moment.

Puzzle gadgets

Pressure plates and doors, mirrors and light locks, pneumatic tubes, cracked floors and more.

Undo, hints and a notebook

Take back any move or start over. Hints come in three steps — a word from Granny Kiyo, the key spots, then a demo (five demos a day; recording a Chain Vault result refills them) — and your notebook keeps the clues.

The hideout museum

Put the stolen parts back together and show them off in your own museum.

The Chain Vault

Once a day, the whole world gets the same new vault. It is built from Solana's first block of the day, so nobody knows what is inside before it opens — not even us.

  1. Every day at 00:00 UTC (9:00 in Japan), a new Chain Vault is born from the first Solana block of the day.
  2. Crack it. Then, if you like, carve your record into Solana from your own wallet. Your move count goes on the chain first; your actual moves stay sealed until the vault closes, so nobody can copy them.
  3. Rankings are built on your own device by reading and checking the records on Solana. There is no OSHO GAMES server in between.
  4. Watch replays of the best solutions, and wear your thief's crest — a picture drawn from your wallet address.
  5. Seeker owners also get a weekly Seeker vault and the ◆ mark.

Connecting a wallet is optional — the whole story plays without one. Recording costs a Solana network fee of about 0.000005 SOL, paid from your wallet; none of it goes to OSHO GAMES. There are no prizes and no tokens: only honour. Records on Solana are public and permanent. After the story, the last door in the museum opens with a free signature. Details: Privacy policy.
