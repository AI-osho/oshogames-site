3. The wallet and the Chain Vault — optional, and never financial

You may connect a Solana wallet. It is entirely optional: the story is complete without it. It is used for the Chain Vault (recording your results, the rankings and the weekly Seeker vault for Seeker owners) and to open the last door in the museum with a signed message.

The game never asks your wallet to send SOL or tokens to anyone. A record is a transaction from your wallet to itself that carries a short memo; the only cost is the network fee. Your wallet shows you every request before you approve it. The game does not process payments and does not use or issue tokens or NFTs. What is sent, and where, is set out in the Privacy Policy.

Records on Solana are public and permanent. Once a record is written, anyone can read it, including your wallet address and, later, your moves. Neither you nor we can change or delete it.

Rankings. Your device builds the rankings itself by reading the public records on Solana. A record only counts once its seal has been opened, after the vault has closed. We run no server and do not referee the rankings, and we cannot remove records from Solana.
